Bio & Curriculum Vitae

Biography

I am a Software Engineering graduate from the University of Sahiwal, Pakistan (CGPA 3.63 / 4.00), with broad interests across artificial intelligence, machine learning, computer vision, natural language processing, and multimodal systems.

Alongside my studies, I have gained hands-on industry experience as a Python developer. At AI 3D Scanning Solution, I worked on text-to-3D and image-to-3D asset generation pipelines, conversational RAG, and semantic product discovery using pgvector with geospatial filtering. Previously at Medtronix Systems / Netso, I built social media and advertising automation systems integrating with the Meta Graph API and Meta Ads Manager, as well as business lead generation web scrapers using Selenium and Playwright.

For my final year project and bachelor's thesis, I developed Validator AI, a multi-agent platform using LangGraph that interviews founders and coordinates 10 concurrent domain modules to generate investor-grade startup validation reports with PostgreSQL state checkpointing.

I am currently preparing for graduate studies (MS / PhD) in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence.
