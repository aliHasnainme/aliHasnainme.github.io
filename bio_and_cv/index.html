Bio & Curriculum Vitae

Biography

I am a software engineer and AI researcher based in Sahiwal, Pakistan. I recently graduated with a BS in Software Engineering from the University of Sahiwal, achieving a CGPA of 3.63 / 4.00. My primary research interest is in machine learning systems—specifically multi-agent architectures, state persistence, and making LLM pipelines resilient against real-world failure modes like schema drift, broken formatting, and state loss.

Throughout my degree, I worked as a software developer in the industry. At Medtronix Systems, I developed asynchronous FastAPI backend microservices and automated invoice parsing pipelines using Google Vision AI and Gemini. At AI 3D Scanning Solution, I designed text-to-3D generation pipelines and engineered a semantic search service combining pgvector, Gemini embeddings, and geospatial filtering.

For my final year capstone and thesis, I created Validator AI, a multi-agent system built on LangGraph that coordinates autonomous agents to interview founders and produce investor-grade startup validation reports. This project helped me tackle practical agent orchestration issues firsthand—such as preventing context collapse, building self-healing output parsers, and enabling session pause-and-resume through PostgreSQL checkpoints.

I am currently preparing research papers based on this work and actively looking for MS and PhD opportunities in Computer Science and AI.

  • Implemented state persistence using a PostgreSQL checkpointer, allowing long-running multi-turn evaluations to be paused, inspected, and resumed across sessions without losing intermediate state.
