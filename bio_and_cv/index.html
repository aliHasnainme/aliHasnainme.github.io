Bio & Curriculum Vitae

Biography

Ali Hasnain is a software engineering graduate from the University of Sahiwal, Pakistan (BS Software Engineering, CGPA 3.63/4.00). His work centers on machine learning and software systems, with an emphasis on stateful multi-agent LLM systems, generative models, and robust error recovery in production AI applications. He has held developer positions at AI 3D Scanning Solution and Medtronix Systems, implementing RAG systems, 3D asset generation workflows, geospatial vector search, and asynchronous backend services in Python and FastAPI.
