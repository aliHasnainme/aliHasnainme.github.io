Bio & Curriculum Vitae

Short Biography

Ali Hasnain is a software engineering researcher and AI engineer specializing in multi-agent LLM systems, generative AI, and reliable machine learning architectures. He earned his BS in Software Engineering from the University of Sahiwal, Pakistan (CGPA 3.63/4.00). His research investigates fault-tolerant agentic workflows, state persistence in cyclic graph systems, and automated schema repair in generative language models. Alongside research, he has built production-grade AI pipelines involving text-to-3D generation, semantic geospatial retrieval, and asynchronous microservices.

Research Experience

Lead Researcher • Validator-AI

2025 – 2026
University of Sahiwal • Supervised Research Project

A Stateful Multi-Agent System for Automated Decision & Startup Validation

  • Designed a cyclic, stateful multi-agent system utilizing LangGraph to overcome severe context-loss and hallucinations observed in conventional stateless prompting.
  • Architected a PostgreSQL-backed state checkpointer enabling interrupted multi-agent workflows to persist state across distributed cycles and recover seamlessly.
  • Implemented an automated self-healing schema repair loop that catches malformed structured outputs, successfully neutralizing 100% of defined schema error patterns in unit testing.
  • Optimized module parallelization using Python's asyncio.gather, decreasing full validation suite execution latency from ~20 minutes to under 3 minutes.
  • Subjected the system to concurrent load tests (Pytest, Playwright, Coverage.py) with up to 100 simulated concurrent actors to stress-test persistence stability.
