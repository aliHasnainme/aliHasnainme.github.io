Ali Hasnain

Software Engineering Graduate • AI & Python Developer

Hi, I'm Ali. I recently graduated with a BS in Software Engineering from the University of Sahiwal, Pakistan (CGPA 3.63 / 4.00). My broad area of interest is artificial intelligence, with a focus spanning machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, natural language processing, and multimodal systems.

Alongside my studies, I have worked professionally as a Python developer. At AI 3D Scanning Solution, I developed text-to-3D and image-to-3D asset generation pipelines and semantic search systems. Previously at Medtronix Systems / Netso, I built social media and advertising automation tools using the Meta Graph API, as well as business lead generation scrapers using Selenium and Playwright.

I am currently preparing for graduate studies (MS / PhD) in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence.

Areas of Interest

  • Computer Vision & 3D Generative AI: Text-to-3D and image-to-3D asset generation pipelines, generative models, and diagnostic deep learning models for medical imaging.
  • Machine Learning & Deep Learning: Neural network architectures, supervised and unsupervised learning, transfer learning, and model evaluation using PyTorch and TensorFlow.
  • Multimodal Systems & Agent Workflows: Graph-based agent orchestration (LangGraph), state persistence with database checkpointers, and self-healing schema validation.
  • Semantic Search & Retrieval: Dense vector retrieval using pgvector, Google Gemini embeddings, and geospatial distance filtering with the Haversine formula.
