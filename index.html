Ali Hasnain

I am a software engineering researcher and AI engineer based in Pakistan, having completed my BS in Software Engineering at the University of Sahiwal (CGPA: 3.63 / 4.00).

Research Focus & Current Status My primary research investigates Stateful Multi-Agent LLM Systems, Generative AI, and Reliable Machine Learning Architectures. I am particularly interested in designing systems that survive state loss, recover deterministically from schema drift, and operate efficiently across asynchronous pipelines. I am currently working on research manuscripts for submission to peer-reviewed venues and preparing for graduate research opportunities.

Research Interests

My work spans modern artificial intelligence and software systems engineering:

  • Multi-Agent Orchestration & State Persistence: Graph-based state machine architectures (LangGraph, AutoGen) that preserve execution context across extended multi-turn reasoning cycles.
  • LLM Reliability & Self-Healing Schemas: Engineering automated repair layers to guarantee strict structural and schema conformance (JSON/Pydantic) in production LLM pipelines.
  • Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) & Spatial Semantics: Dense vector retrieval using Gemini embeddings in pgvector, integrated with geodesic and geospatial filtering algorithms.
  • Generative Multimodal Models: Diffusion models and text-to-3D asset generation pipelines.

Flagship Research Project
Validator-AI: A Stateful Multi-Agent System for Automated Startup Validation
Ali Hasnain — University of Sahiwal (2025–2026)

A multi-agent LLM framework designed to eliminate context collapse during complex multi-step reasoning. Built with a PostgreSQL-backed state checkpointer to allow interrupted workflows to resume seamlessly, coupled with an automated self-healing validation layer catching 100% of schema failures. Re-architected with asyncio.gather to drop end-to-end execution latency from ~20 minutes to under 3 minutes, verified up to 100 concurrent users.
